# Brand name candidates

Target: Lithuania first, playful, speaking to kids and parents. None chosen yet; decided together with logo, mascot and tone of voice in Milestone 1.

**A. When it happens**
1. **Popiet** — "in the afternoon", when clubs happen
2. **Varpelis** — the little school bell; after it rings, the fun starts
3. **Po skambučio** → **Poskambis** — "after the bell", shortened
4. **Laisvu** — from *laisvu laiku* ("in free time")

**B. Trying things / curiosity**
5. **Pabandyk** — "give it a try!"
6. **Dar!** — "more!", what kids shout
7. **Daryk** — "do it / make it"
8. **Kodėl** — "why?", the curious-kid question
9. **Ateik** — "come along"

**C. Lithuanian folklore and imagery**
10. **Aitvaras** — a kite, and the folklore spirit that brings luck; flying, colourful, mascot-ready
11. **Laumė** — the folk fairy who teaches crafts and spinning
12. **Kompasas** — a compass that helps find your hobby
13. **Raketa** — a rocket, pure kid energy
14. **Spalvelė** — "little colour"

**D. Invented, short, works in LT and EN**
15. **Hobi** — "hobby", said the Lithuanian way
16. **Mokis** — from *mokytis* ("to learn"), with a cute ending
17. **Žaismas → Zaisma** — from *žaisti* ("to play")
18. **Bimba**
19. **Lunko**
20. **Tikru** — from *tikrai!* ("for real!")
21. **Joti** — from *joti* ("to ride"), a fast ride to activities

**E. Mixed LT/EN SaaS style**
22. **Klubis** — "club" with a Lithuanian ending
23. **Hopa!** — "hop!", what you say when jumping
24. **Afterbell**
25. **Kidsy**
26. **Grupė+**

**F. Warm and parent-friendly**
27. **Šypsena** — "smile"
28. **Kartu** — "together"
29. **Mūsų būrelis → Musu** — "our club"
30. **Savaitė** — "the week", everything a kid does in one week
